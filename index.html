My Waypoints - Your GPS tracks on a live map


This web-based application is perfect for hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, wildlife excursions, bicycling adventures, boaters, and anyone who wants to see their movement on a live map which can be saved and shared with friends and family.


Here's how it works:


On your next adventure, from where you want to start recording your path, press the start recording button and then press the red snap button to take your first GPS waypoint. Each time you find an interesting landmark, or make a change in direction, press the red snap button again. Continue this process until you reach your destination and then press the stop recording button after taking your last GPS waypoint.


Once recording is stopped, go to the app menu, and under saved sessions you'll find your recorded sessions. Open this saved session and then click on notes. From here you'll be able to add notes to each waypoint. And, you can make this route public and copy the shareable URL link with friends and family. Lastly, on your next hike in this local area, you can pull up the saved track file and navigate back to landmarks, or re-trace your steps.


Other uses:


  • 1. Add waypoints to the live map using known GPS latitude and longitude coordinates.
  • 2. When not recording, press the red snap button to add your current location.
  • 3. On recorded sessions, seeing distance between recorded points on the map.
  • 4. Seeing elevation information.

Map Areas


Map areas (visualized as blue circles on the global map) are broad areas users can add to the global map to add information and open the area to application user's comments and pictures. Map areas can be made private (not shown on the map), or public. Map areas are intended for places like cities, large bodies of water, trails, mountain ranges, lakes, and areas within the ocean.


Pinned Locations


Pinned areas created with the red snap button or manually adding via coordinates are small specific locations users can add to the global map to add information and open the specific location to user's comments and pictures. Pinned locations can be made private (not shown on the map), or public. Pinned locations are intended for places like restaurants, landmarks, businesses, and places of interest in cities, specific locations within large bodies of water, landmarks on trails, places of interest on a mountain range trail, fishing areas on lakes, and diving locations within the ocean to explore shipwrecks.

Frequently asked questions


1. Does this web app show a line connecting a set of waypoints so I can visually see a line on a map?


Yes, on My Waypoints, when you start recording and press the red snap button to add your current location to the session and you arrive at your destination and stop recording, our application will connect the points on the map with a visual line and overlay it on the map. Then, at a later date you can open the route and follow the line to travel the exact route you did when you did the initial session.


2. Does this application measure distance and elevation?


Yes, My Waypoints can measure the distance between two points on the map when recording a route, and elevation is always available.
