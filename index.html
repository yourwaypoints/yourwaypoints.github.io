My Waypoints - Your GPS tracks on a live map.

This web-based application is perfect for hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, wildlife excursions, bicycling adventures, boaters, and anyone who wants to see their movement on a live map that can be saved and shared with friends and family.

Here is how it works:

On your next adventure, from where you want to record all steps, press the start recording button and then press the red snap button to take your first GPS waypoint. Each time you find an interesting landmark, or make a change in direction, press the red snap button again. Continue this process until you reach your destination and then press the stop recording button.

Once recording is stopped, go to the app menu, and under saved sessions you will find your recorded tracks. Open this saved session and then click on notes. From here you'll be able to add notes to each waypoint. And, you can make this route public and copy the shareable URL link with friends and family.

Other uses:

  • Add waypoints to the live map using known GPS latitude and longitude coordinates.
  • While not recording, press the red snap button to add your current location.
